# The Salvation Army, Southwest Division

Is an FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

The Salvation Army, Southwest Division serves communities across Arizona, New Mexico, and Southern Nevada through a wide range of social services, spiritual support, emergency assistance, and community programs. The division is part of The Salvation Army's broader international movement and works locally to meet human needs without discrimination.

The Southwest Division operates nearly 60 units across the region and provides support for people facing poverty, homelessness, hunger, disaster, addiction, family instability, and other hardships. Its services include food assistance, emergency shelter, clothing, rent and utility assistance, disaster relief, heat relief, senior activity and outreach, youth programs, adult rehabilitation, holiday assistance, and emotional and spiritual care.

In the Phoenix area, The Salvation Army provides services through its Herberger Campus and other local programs. These include family services, emergency shelter, veteran services, homeless outreach, extreme heat relief, youth programs, senior programs, Pathway of Hope, emergency disaster services, and the Adult Rehabilitation Center. The organization also operates Family Thrift Stores, where donated goods help support recovery and rehabilitation programs.

The division's food and hunger relief work includes pantries, hot meals, groceries, and mobile or community-based distribution. Its homelessness and housing services include emergency and transitional shelter, case management, and practical support for individuals and families in crisis. Its family and youth services include after-school programs, summer activities, music programs, mentoring, holiday assistance, and community center programming.

The Salvation Army Southwest Division also responds to disasters and extreme weather events with immediate relief and longer-term recovery support. In Arizona, its heat relief work is especially important during dangerous summer temperatures, when outreach teams and service sites help provide water, cooling support, and connection to additional resources.

## Mission & Vision

The Salvation Army's mission is to preach the gospel of Jesus Christ and to meet human needs in His name without discrimination.

The Southwest Division carries out that mission by serving people in Arizona, New Mexico, and Southern Nevada through practical assistance, social services, spiritual care, and community partnerships. Its work is rooted in the belief that people should receive help with dignity, compassion, and hope, whether they need food, shelter, recovery support, emergency assistance, disaster relief, or long-term pathways toward self-sufficiency.

The organization frames its public service around "Doing the Most Good" and focuses on helping the most people in the most need. Across its programs, The Salvation Army emphasizes compassion, faith-driven service, stewardship, emergency response, family stability, recovery, and support for people working to move out of crisis.
