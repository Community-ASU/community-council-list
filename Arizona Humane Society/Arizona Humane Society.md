# Arizona Humane Society

Not an FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

Arizona Humane Society, commonly known as AHS, is a local, private nonprofit animal welfare organization serving pets and people in the Valley. The organization is not affiliated with other humane societies and depends on private donations to support its lifesaving work for sick, injured, abused, and homeless animals.

AHS focuses on saving the community's most vulnerable animals and enriching the lives of pets and people. Its work includes animal rescue, sheltering, adoption, foster care, veterinary care, trauma care, spay and neuter services, pet behavior support, humane education, and resources that help keep pets with the families who love them.

The organization operates specialized lifesaving programs for animals that may otherwise be at risk in traditional shelter settings. These include Emergency Animal Medical Technicians, a trauma hospital, Bottle Baby ICU, Parvo ICU, and other medical and shelter programs that care for injured, neglected, orphaned, and medically fragile pets.

AHS also supports the human-animal bond through adoption services, lost and found pet resources, affordable veterinary services, volunteer opportunities, education, pet training, community events, and assistance for pet owners facing barriers to care. Its programs are designed not only to rescue animals in crisis, but also to reduce preventable suffering and strengthen responsible pet ownership across the community.

Through its shelter, veterinary, rescue, foster, adoption, thrift store, volunteer, and donor-supported programs, Arizona Humane Society works to create a more compassionate future for animals in Arizona. The organization emphasizes lifesaving innovation, access to care, community support, and advocacy for animals who are sick, injured, abused, or without homes.

## Mission & Vision

Arizona Humane Society's mission is to save the most vulnerable animals and enrich the lives of pets and people.

The organization carries out that mission by providing lifesaving medical care, shelter, adoption, rescue, foster support, behavioral resources, spay and neuter services, and community programs for animals and the people who care for them.

AHS's vision is reflected in its commitment to transform animal welfare by reducing preventable euthanasia, caring for animals with complex medical and behavioral needs, strengthening the bond between pets and people, and creating a community where vulnerable animals have the chance to heal, find homes, and thrive.
