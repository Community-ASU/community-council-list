# Terros Health

Not an FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

Terros Health is a Phoenix-based community health organization that has served Arizona since 1969. The organization provides integrated, whole-person care that combines primary care, behavioral health, substance use treatment, psychiatry, prevention, crisis response, and supportive services in community settings across the state.

Terros Health presents itself as a mission-driven health system focused on helping people become the best versions of themselves. Its care model is designed to meet patients where they are, whether they need a routine primary care visit, counseling, addiction treatment, or help navigating barriers such as housing, food, employment, or transportation. The organization emphasizes that care should be accessible regardless of a person's ability to pay.

The organization operates community health centers, residential treatment programs, mobile and community-based services, and partnerships with schools, courts, Boys & Girls Clubs, and other neighborhood settings. Its approach centers on integrating physical health, behavioral health, and social needs so patients can get support in one connected system.

Terros Health also emphasizes workforce culture and community impact. Its public materials describe a values-driven organization where compassion, purpose, and innovation guide care delivery and employee development.

## Mission & Vision

Terros Health's mission is to help people become the best versions of themselves.

Its vision is extraordinary care by empowered employees achieving exceptional outcomes.

The organization's values are compassion, integrity, and empowerment. Those values show up in Terros Health's emphasis on whole-person care, trust-building, and removing barriers so people can access the right care at the right time. The mission and vision reflect a broader commitment to health, healing, and community well-being across Arizona.
