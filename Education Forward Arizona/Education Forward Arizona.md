# Education Forward Arizona

Not an FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

Education Forward Arizona is a statewide nonprofit focused on aligning Arizona's education, workforce, and economic goals. The organization advocates for high-quality education and training pathways that connect learners to career and workforce opportunities and improve quality of life for Arizonans.

The organization presents itself as a bridge between educators, employers, policymakers, and communities. Its work is rooted in partnership and systems-level change, with an emphasis on bringing people together around shared priorities so Arizona learners can move through stronger and more coherent pathways from early learning through postsecondary education and into the workforce.

Education Forward Arizona also serves as a nonpartisan voice on education and workforce alignment. Its public materials highlight a focus on data, policy, storytelling, and convening, all aimed at expanding access to degrees, certificates, and credentials that support high-wage, high-growth, sustainable careers.

The organization says it is working toward Arizona's statewide attainment goal, while also supporting tools, scholarship programs, and collaborative initiatives that help more students and adult learners complete education beyond high school. Its broader message is that education is foundational not only to individual opportunity but also to Arizona's economic future.

## Mission & Vision

Education Forward Arizona's mission is to advocate for high-quality education and training that aligns with career and workforce opportunities to advance the quality of life for all Arizonans.

Its vision by 2030 is that all Arizonans, regardless of background, have access to flexible, high-quality, and integrated education and training pathways that prepare them with the necessary skills and knowledge to thrive in a rapidly changing environment and help strengthen Arizona's economy.

The organization's mission and vision emphasize access, alignment, and measurable progress. Rather than focusing on a single program area, Education Forward Arizona works across the education continuum and with multiple sectors to improve outcomes at scale. Its strategy reflects a belief that Arizona's long-term prosperity depends on better connecting education to opportunity and ensuring learners have the support they need to persist and succeed.
