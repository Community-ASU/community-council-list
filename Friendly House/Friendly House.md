# Friendly House

FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

Friendly House is one of Arizona's pioneer social service agencies. Established in 1920, it began as a local initiative to help immigrants build the citizenship, education, and literacy skills needed to acculturate into the United States. More than 100 years later, the organization has evolved into a transformational, multi-service nonprofit serving the diverse needs of the Phoenix community.

The organization's work spans education, workforce development, family support, basic needs, and immigration services. Its public materials describe a broad service model that includes a K-8 charter school, adult education and workforce development, early childhood education, family and youth services, immigration and legal services, refugee programs, food pantry support, and other community-facing resources. Friendly House says it offers more than 56 programs in support of its mission.

Friendly House positions itself as a place where individuals and families can access the tools, education, and support they need to move forward. Its services are designed to help people learn English, pursue education, get better jobs, become U.S. citizens, meet basic family needs, and navigate college and career pathways. The organization frames this work as a way to help residents participate fully in the American dream and break cycles of poverty.

## Mission & Vision

Friendly House's mission is empowering Arizona communities through education and human services.

Its public vision is expressed through the idea of launching lives further and empowering communities to achieve the American dream through impactful, life-changing services. The organization emphasizes confidence, independence, and self-sufficiency as outcomes of its programs.

The mission is reflected across its services: adult education, family support, legal services, refugee support, and early childhood programming all work together to help participants build stability and opportunity. Friendly House also highlights a core value of listening, acting, learning, and repeating as part of its broader commitment to community-centered service.
