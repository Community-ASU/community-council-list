# Valley of the Sun United Way

Not an FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

Valley of the Sun United Way, commonly known as VSUW, is a Phoenix-based nonprofit that brings together donors, volunteers, businesses, nonprofit organizations, schools, public agencies, and community leaders to improve lives across Maricopa County. For more than 100 years, the organization has united people and institutions to address major community challenges and create lasting change.

VSUW serves Maricopa County and focuses its community impact work through its Mighty Change 2026 plan. The plan concentrates the organization's efforts in four major areas: education, housing and homelessness, health, and workforce development. Through this framework, Valley of the Sun United Way identifies emerging needs, convenes partners, invests in proven solutions, mobilizes volunteers and donors, and supports collaborative strategies designed to improve outcomes across the region.

The organization's education work focuses on helping children and youth succeed in school, life, and work. Its goals include increasing third grade reading proficiency and increasing the number of youth ages 16 to 24 who are engaged in education and employment opportunities. VSUW works with schools, nonprofits, community partners, and volunteers to strengthen learning, opportunity, and youth development.

VSUW's housing and homelessness work focuses on reducing homelessness and supporting systems that help individuals and families move from crisis toward stability. Its health work includes efforts to reduce food insecurity and increase access to affordable healthcare. Its workforce development work focuses on preparing people for living-wage jobs and helping individuals achieve higher-paying employment.

Valley of the Sun United Way also supports the community through strategic investments, grantmaking, volunteer engagement, donor-designated giving, public-private partnerships, pilot projects, advocacy, emergency response, disaster preparedness, and convening work. The organization describes its role as more than fundraising; it brings partners together to create collaborative solutions that transform individual lives and strengthen entire communities.

## Mission & Vision

Valley of the Sun United Way's mission is to improve lives by mobilizing the caring power of the community.

The organization envisions a community where every child, family, and individual is healthy, has a safe place to live, and has every opportunity to succeed in school, in work, and in life.

Through its Mighty Change work, VSUW aims to build a more equitable and thriving future for Maricopa County by aligning resources, partners, volunteers, donors, and community-informed strategies around bold goals in education, housing and homelessness, health, and workforce development.
