# Hope Women's Center

Not an FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

Hope Women's Center is a faith-based nonprofit serving women and teen girls across Arizona who are facing crisis, instability, trauma, or major life transitions. The organization describes its work as guiding women from trauma to transformation through relational, holistic support that addresses physical, emotional, and spiritual needs.

Founded in 1984 as a small crisis pregnancy center in Apache Junction, Hope has expanded into a statewide support network. Its current footprint includes centers in Phoenix, Apache Junction, Coolidge, Maricopa, Northern Arizona, South Phoenix, and West Phoenix, along with Hope on the Road mobile and partner-site programming that brings services into additional Arizona communities.

Hope provides a safe place for at-risk women and teen girls to receive free support, practical resources, and encouragement. Its services include life-skills classes, support groups, one-on-one mentoring, counseling, advocacy, crisis referrals, parenting and pregnancy support, job coaching, and material assistance such as clothing, food, household items, toiletries, diapers, and utility help. The organization also operates Every Mother's Advocate programming for mothers in crisis.

The center's model is trauma-informed and designed for women dealing with a wide range of difficult circumstances, including domestic abuse, sexual abuse, substance use disorder, homelessness, unemployment, poverty, unplanned pregnancy, grief, mental health concerns, family conflict, parenting stress, trafficking, and involvement with child welfare systems. Hope emphasizes safety, dignity, relationship, and long-term stability rather than one-time intervention.

Hope Women's Center is supported through individuals, churches, civic groups, businesses, grants, foundations, volunteers, and community partners. Its Arizona headquarters is at 1640 E. McDowell Road in Phoenix, and the organization is registered as a 501(c)(3) nonprofit with EIN 86-0668354.

## Mission & Vision

Hope Women's Center's mission is to engage, encourage, and equip vulnerable women and teen girls by providing support for their physical, emotional, and spiritual needs.

The organization's practical vision is reflected in its guiding message that no woman should face a crisis alone. Hope frames its work as helping women move from trauma to transformation by creating safe relationships, encouraging women to recognize their strength and value, and equipping them with tools to break unhealthy patterns and make healthy choices.

Its statewide growth strategy is centered on "One Mission. One State. More Hope." Through physical centers, mobile programming, and partner sites, Hope seeks to make help available closer to where women live, especially in underserved communities. The organization combines spiritual care, emotional support, education, advocacy, and practical aid so that women and teen girls can stabilize, heal, and move forward.
