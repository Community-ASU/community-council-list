# Read Better Be Better

FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

Read Better Be Better is a Phoenix-based 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization focused on literacy, youth leadership, and educational equity in Arizona. The organization was founded in 2014 by Sophie Allen-Etchart in response to Arizona's literacy crisis and the long-term academic risk faced by students who are not reading proficiently by the end of third grade.

The organization centers its work on the transition from "learning to read" to "reading to learn." Students who struggle with reading by fourth grade often face greater barriers across every subject area, so Read Better Be Better intervenes early by building reading fluency, comprehension, concentration, confidence, and enjoyment of reading.

Read Better Be Better began as an afterschool reading comprehension and leadership program that paired younger readers with middle school student leaders. Its model uses peer-led learning: older students receive training and support so they can coach younger students through structured literacy activities. That approach gives younger students more individualized reading support while helping older students practice leadership, communication, responsibility, and service.

The organization has expanded beyond its original program into a broader "Changemaker Continuum" serving students from kindergarten through 12th grade. Young readers build foundational literacy skills; fourth through eighth grade students develop leadership by helping younger students read; and high school students participate in pathway programs that connect literacy, education careers, civic leadership, and community engagement.

Its Pathways to Education Professions Program gives high school students hands-on experience in education by placing them in classroom settings within K-8 after-school literacy programs. Its Pathways to Civic Leadership Program helps high school students strengthen leadership skills, understand community issues, and see themselves as changemakers who can participate in improving Arizona's education system.

Read Better Be Better's work is rooted in the idea that literacy is foundational to success in education, leadership, and life. The organization helps students build the academic and personal skills needed to participate more fully in school, community, and future civic life.

## Mission & Vision

Read Better Be Better's mission is to equip youth with the skills to read and lead.

The organization also describes its mission as connecting young readers and youth leaders to inspire a love of literacy and learning. Its program model is designed to improve reading fluency and comprehension while developing confidence, leadership, and a sense of agency among the older students who serve as leaders.

Read Better Be Better's vision is a world in which empowered communities demand equitable access to foundational skills. The organization views literacy as a key to that future: when students can read, they can better understand themselves, their communities, and the world around them; when reading is inaccessible, learning and leadership opportunities become harder to reach.

Its guiding progression is that Readers become Leaders, and Leaders become Changemakers. Through that continuum, Read Better Be Better aims to make literacy support part of a larger pathway toward youth leadership, educational opportunity, and community change.
