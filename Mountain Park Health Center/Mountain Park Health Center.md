# Mountain Park Health Center

Not an FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

Mountain Park Health Center is a nonprofit community health center that has served the Phoenix metro area for more than 40 years. The organization provides affordable primary care and whole-patient care for entire families in Goodyear, Glendale, Tempe, and Phoenix, and it says it keeps nearly 115,000 neighbors healthy each year.

As a patient-centered medical home, Mountain Park offers primary care, behavioral health, clinical pharmacy, dietetics, and other services in one place. The organization emphasizes preventive care, screenings, access to affordable medications, and help navigating AHCCCS, marketplace coverage, SNAP, and TANF. Its model is built around keeping healthcare affordable and making care accessible to people who might otherwise struggle to get it.

Mountain Park also highlights its role as a community health partner. Its public materials stress quality, safety, and employee education, and the organization points to Joint Commission accreditation as evidence of its commitment to standards and continuous improvement. Across its clinics, Mountain Park serves diverse communities with bilingual, affordable, coordinated care that is designed to support patients over time, not just during a single visit.

## Mission & Vision

Mountain Park Health Center's mission is to improve the health of the communities it serves by providing high quality, affordable healthcare.

Its public vision is to keep communities healthy through proactive, patient-centered, accessible care. The organization believes in helping people stay well before problems become serious, while also making it easier to manage chronic conditions and get support for the practical barriers that often stand between people and good health.

The mission is reflected in Mountain Park's emphasis on whole-person care, affordable access, and coordinated services. The organization views healthcare as a long-term community responsibility and strives to create a welcoming place where patients can get the care they need and feel supported by their care team.
