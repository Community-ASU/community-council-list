# Hope Women's Center

Not an FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

Hope Women’s Center serves women and teen girls across Arizona who are facing crisis, instability, or a major life transition. The organization describes its work as helping women move from trauma toward transformation through relational, holistic support.

Its mission is to engage, encourage, and equip vulnerable women and teen girls by supporting their physical, emotional, and spiritual needs. That support includes classes, support groups, mentoring, counseling, advocacy, and practical assistance such as material and utility help.

Founded in 1984 as a small crisis pregnancy center in Apache Junction, Hope has grown into a network of centers in Phoenix, Northern Arizona, Coolidge, Maricopa, South Phoenix, and the West Valley. The Hope on the Road program extends that reach through partner sites across Arizona so women can access help closer to where they live.

The center is faith-based and supported through partnerships with individuals, churches, civic groups, businesses, grants, foundations, and other community partners. Across the organization, the emphasis is on making women feel safe, heard, and supported while they build stability and forward momentum.
