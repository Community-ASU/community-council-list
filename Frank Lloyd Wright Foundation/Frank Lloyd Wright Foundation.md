# Frank Lloyd Wright Foundation

Not an FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

The Frank Lloyd Wright Foundation is dedicated to preserving and promoting the architectural legacy of Frank Lloyd Wright and advancing the broader impact of architecture and design. Based in Scottsdale and anchored at Taliesin West, the Foundation preserves the buildings, landscapes, and collections associated with Wright's homes and studios while also connecting the public to the continuing relevance of his ideas.

The Foundation describes its work across several dimensions: preserving Taliesin and Taliesin West, influencing architecture and design through collaboration and scholarship, and educating the public through tours, exhibits, performances, school programs, and digital engagement. It also manages licensing activities that extend Wright-inspired design into broader public life.

The organization's public materials emphasize Wright's enduring relevance and the Foundation's role in helping people discover how his ideas still shape how we live, learn, and build. Through preservation, interpretation, and programming, the Foundation presents itself as both a steward of historic sites and a living institution advancing design education.

## Mission & Vision

The Frank Lloyd Wright Foundation's mission is to connect people to the continued relevance of Frank Lloyd Wright's architectural legacy; preserve the buildings, landscapes, and collections of Taliesin and Taliesin West; and advance the impact of architecture and design.

Its vision is a better world shaped by Frank Lloyd Wright's legacy and ideas, inspiring how we live, learn, and build.

The Foundation frames its work as turning Wright's philosophy into action for a thriving future. That includes preserving historic spaces, supporting education and scholarship, and encouraging people to think about architecture as something that can improve daily life and the built environment.
