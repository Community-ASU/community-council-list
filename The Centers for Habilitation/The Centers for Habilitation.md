# The Centers for Habilitation

Not an FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

The Centers for Habilitation, commonly known as TCH, is an Arizona nonprofit that creates opportunities for individuals with physical and developmental disabilities. The organization provides programs and supports intended to help people live more independent, connected, and fulfilling lives.

TCH serves adults with disabilities through a broad set of support services, including day programs, home and community-based services, group homes, employment services, transportation, and AbilityOne contract work. Its programs are designed to support people in daily living, community participation, skill development, employment readiness, and self-direction.

TCH's day programs combine social, recreational, and educational opportunities so adults with developmental and physical disabilities can broaden their social connections and become more independent citizens. Its home and community-based services support individuals living in their own homes, with family members, or in other community settings by helping with personal care, decision-making, goal planning, budgeting, meals, respite, and community integration.

The organization also provides group home services in Maricopa and Pima counties and operates accessible transportation services for supported individuals. Its employment services include job readiness, job development, job placement, job retention, supported employment, group supported employment, post-employment assistance, and work adjustment training.

Through AbilityOne and other enterprise activities, TCH connects people with significant disabilities to employment opportunities. The organization also operates ASDD Document Destruction, an Arizona secure data destruction service and division of TCH.

TCH frames its work around respect, inspiration, adaptability, accountability, and integrity. Across its programs, the organization emphasizes dignity, individual choice, inclusion, health and wellness, self-confidence, and practical support that helps people overcome barriers and improve self-sufficiency.

## Mission & Vision

TCH's mission is to create opportunities for individuals with disabilities.

The organization describes its mission as empowering individuals with physical and developmental disabilities to live fulfilling lives by providing individualized programs and supports tailored to their unique needs. TCH strives to encourage health and wellness, promote inclusion in community life, and increase self-esteem and confidence by ensuring each individual has the opportunity to make their own choices and direct their own path.

Its vision is reflected in its belief that Arizona's adults with disabilities thrive best when they have access to the same opportunities, attention, support, dignity, equality, comfort, and independence that all people deserve.
