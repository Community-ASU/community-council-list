# Junior Achievement of Arizona

FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

Junior Achievement of Arizona, commonly known as JA Arizona, is a nonprofit education organization that prepares Arizona learners for success in work and life. The organization connects classroom learning to real-world experience through programs focused on financial literacy, career readiness, entrepreneurship, workforce development, and economic mobility.

JA Arizona is headquartered in Tempe and has an additional office in Tucson serving Southern Arizona. As part of the Junior Achievement USA network, the organization works with educators, schools, families, businesses, community volunteers, and partners across the state to help young people build the knowledge, skills, confidence, and networks needed for future success.

The organization serves learners from kindergarten through post-secondary education. Its programs include in-classroom instruction, hands-on experiential learning, virtual learning, career exploration, and instructional models that connect students to real business and community mentors. JA Arizona reaches more than 150,000 Arizona learners each year, supported by thousands of business and community volunteers.

JA Arizona's core program areas include JA BizTown, JA Finance Park, JA Money in Motion, JA Inspire, classroom-based programs, online resources, and 3DE. JA BizTown gives fourth through sixth grade students a student-sized town where they learn economic concepts and workplace skills. JA Finance Park helps students explore budgeting, personal finance, and adult decision-making. JA Money in Motion brings financial planning simulations to schools, including rural communities. JA Inspire provides career exploration and workforce readiness experiences for learners ages 12 to 25.

The organization also supports young people through volunteer mentoring, student leadership opportunities, career-connected learning, free online resources, and partnerships with schools and employers. Its work is intended to close the gap between classroom learning and the skills, experiences, and networks students need to thrive in a changing economy.

## Mission & Vision

Junior Achievement of Arizona's mission is to prepare Arizona learners, K-25, to succeed in work and life.

The organization is inspired by the belief that education should open doors to opportunity. JA Arizona works to help students connect learning to life, build confidence in their futures, and develop the financial literacy, career readiness, and entrepreneurial skills needed for long-term success.

JA Arizona's broader vision is reflected in its commitment to expanding pathways to economic mobility for young people across the state. By bringing together educators, volunteers, businesses, and communities, the organization aims to strengthen Arizona's future workforce, economy, and communities.
