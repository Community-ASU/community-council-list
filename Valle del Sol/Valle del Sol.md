# Valle del Sol

Not an FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

Valle del Sol Community Health is a federally qualified health center and nonprofit community health organization dedicated to transforming lives across the greater Phoenix Valley. Founded in 1970 by social justice activists, the organization grew out of a civil rights and community advocacy tradition focused on closing gaps in behavioral health and social support for Latino and underserved communities.

Today, Valle del Sol provides integrated medical and behavioral health services to adults, children, seniors, and families from many backgrounds. Its care model includes primary care, pediatrics, women's health, behavioral health, substance use recovery, psychiatry, and wellness services. The organization also offers culturally centered, bilingual care and sliding-fee support so cost is less of a barrier for patients.

Valle del Sol presents itself as more than a health care provider. Its public materials emphasize community empowerment, leadership development, and advocacy. The organization says it is still fighting for the community that built it, and it uses programs such as the Hispanic Leadership Institute and Profiles of Success to celebrate and grow Latino leadership across Arizona.

The organization is also expanding access through mobile health units and a growing clinic network across the Phoenix metropolitan area. Its public narrative centers on meeting people where they are, improving health outcomes, and addressing the broader factors that shape well-being.

## Mission & Vision

Valle del Sol Community Health's mission is to transform lives through compassionate, culturally centered care that empowers individuals and communities. The organization also says it is dedicated to fostering a new generation of diverse, visionary leaders who champion equity, inspire progress, and drive lasting positive change.

Its vision is to inspire people to live healthy, empowered, and productive lives.

The organization's values are integrity and honesty, diversity, commitment, teamwork, excellence, and respect. These values shape its approach to care, leadership development, and community advocacy. Taken together, they reflect a mission that combines health services with empowerment, equity, and long-term community investment.
