# Catholic Charities Community Services

FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

Catholic Charities Community Services is a faith-based nonprofit serving vulnerable people and families in central and northern Arizona. Founded in 1933, the organization says it helps children, adults, families, and communities live with independence and dignity through a wide range of programs and services.

Its work is grounded in Catholic social teaching and a strong service ethic. The organization describes its mission as helping the community's most vulnerable with solutions that permanently improve lives, and its public materials emphasize love, hope, compassion, dignity, and self-sufficiency. Catholic Charities frames its services as both immediate help and long-term support, recognizing that people often need both crisis response and sustained accompaniment.

The organization operates across several service areas, including housing and shelter, counseling, domestic violence support, early education, family stability, foster care and adoption, homelessness services, immigration legal services, medical loan closet assistance, pregnancy support, private infant adoption, refugee services, sex trafficking response, veterans services, youth development, and community outreach. Its public materials also note that it serves thousands of people each year and works across southern Arizona through a network of programs and partners.

Catholic Charities describes itself as a diverse family of clients, staff, volunteers, and donors. That language reflects its community-wide approach: partner broadly, serve respectfully, and build practical pathways toward stability. Its website also highlights a holistic model that combines case management, behavioral health, and supportive services to help people move toward greater safety and well-being.

## Mission & Vision

Catholic Charities Community Services' vision is a compassionate and just community that upholds the God-given worth and dignity of every human being.

Its mission is to strengthen children, adults, families and communities by providing help, creating hope and serving all.

The organization's values in action include responding to the Gospel call to care for neighbors, respecting life and vulnerable people, collaborating with others to meet unmet needs, cultivating creative responses to community challenges, respecting diversity, encouraging self-reliance, advocating for a just and compassionate society, and maintaining integrity and accountability.

Taken together, those principles make the mission broader than any single program. Catholic Charities aims to meet urgent needs, build stability, and support long-term flourishing while staying rooted in faith-based service and community partnership.
