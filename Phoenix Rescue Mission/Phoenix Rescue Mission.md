# Phoenix Rescue Mission

Not an FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

Phoenix Rescue Mission is a Christian nonprofit serving people across the Valley who are facing hunger, homelessness, addiction, trauma, and other life-controlling crises. The organization provides comprehensive, Christ-centered care designed to address immediate needs while helping individuals and families move toward long-term stability and transformation.

The Mission's core work includes residential recovery programs for men, women, and women with children. These programs address physical, emotional, financial, and spiritual needs so participants can work toward recovery, independence, and productive community life. Its Transformations program provides a year-long path for people seeking recovery and freedom from addiction, while its Rescue-Assess-Place program offers short-term residential crisis intervention and case management to help people identify the next right program or service.

Phoenix Rescue Mission also operates homeless outreach through Hope Coach vehicles that travel throughout Maricopa County to reach people living on the streets. These teams provide water, basic necessities, connection, and case management support directly where people are. During the summer, the Mission's Code:Red initiative focuses on heat relief by distributing water and providing lifesaving outreach to people at risk during extreme temperatures.

The organization supports food assistance through Hope for Hunger Food Bank in Glendale and mobile pantry services that bring nutritious food and household essentials to underserved areas of the Valley. It also provides specialized services such as work programs, criminal justice diversion, housing assistance, job readiness, job placement support, education, and vocational pathways for people rebuilding their lives.

Phoenix Rescue Mission traces its history back more than 70 years and has grown into a broad service organization with residential recovery, outreach, food assistance, and specialized support programs. Its work is funded largely by individuals, churches, businesses, foundations, and other community partners.

## Mission & Vision

Phoenix Rescue Mission's mission is to provide Christ-centered, life-transforming solutions to people facing hunger, homelessness, addiction, and trauma.

Its vision is love, hope, and transformation to every neighbor in the Valley.

The organization frames its work through values rooted in Christian faith, love and respect for others, going the extra mile, and stewarding resources wisely. Across its programs, Phoenix Rescue Mission emphasizes dignity, compassion, recovery, practical support, and long-term transformation for people experiencing crisis.
