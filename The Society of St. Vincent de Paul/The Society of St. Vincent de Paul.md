# The Society of St. Vincent de Paul

Is an FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

The Society of St. Vincent de Paul, Phoenix Diocesan Council, is an Arizona nonprofit serving individuals and families across central and northern Arizona who need help with food, clothing, housing, healthcare, and other basic needs. The organization combines direct services with volunteer-driven neighborhood support, creating practical pathways for people who have nowhere else to turn for help.

St. Vincent de Paul has served Arizona since 1946. Its work is rooted in the Catholic Vincentian tradition of neighbors helping neighbors, but the organization describes its services as common ground for people of all backgrounds to find compassion, meaning, joy, and hope. Today, SVdP operates a wide range of programs through campus-based services and more than 80 Conferences of Charity led by trained volunteers.

The organization's services include charity dining rooms, food boxes for families, thrift stores, homelessness prevention, housing and shelter support, medical and dental care for uninsured patients, companion animal services, and general assistance for people experiencing poverty. Its Phoenix-area campuses include dining rooms, clinics, farms, shelter and housing programs, and community spaces that support direct service and volunteer engagement.

SVdP's dining rooms and food programs serve thousands of meals and distribute food through community partners and neighborhood-based support. Its medical and dental clinics provide care for uninsured patients, while its housing and shelter programs support vulnerable unhoused individuals and families working toward stability. Through its volunteer conferences, Vincentians visit neighbors in need, provide assistance, and connect people to available resources.

The organization also invests in community engagement, service, and advocacy. Its newer Center for Purpose, Hope and Mission is intended to expand SVdP's capacity to bring people together for volunteerism, learning, mission-driven programming, and community-wide collaboration.

## Mission & Vision

The Society of St. Vincent de Paul's mission is to feed, clothe, house, and heal individuals and families in the community who have nowhere else to turn for help, while also providing meaningful opportunities for volunteers to serve their neighbors in need with love and compassion.

Its vision is reflected in its belief that St. Vincent de Paul is for everyone, a common ground of service and compassion where people can find meaning, joy, and hope.

Across its work, SVdP emphasizes the intrinsic value of every person, the dignity of serving and being served, and the power of community partnerships to bring wholeness and healing. The organization frames its work around compassion, service, volunteerism, practical assistance, and care for neighbors experiencing poverty, hunger, homelessness, illness, and crisis.
