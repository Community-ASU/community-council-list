# Teach For America Phoenix

Not an FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

Teach For America Phoenix is the Phoenix regional presence of Teach For America, a national education and leadership-development nonprofit. The Phoenix region works to expand educational opportunity in Arizona by recruiting, developing, and supporting teachers, tutors, alumni, and community leaders who serve students in under-resourced schools and communities.

Teach For America has worked in Phoenix since 1994. The region has brought more than 2,500 teachers to the area over that time and now includes a large local alumni network. In 2025, Teach For America Phoenix reported more than 1,200 alumni living and working in the region, along with more than 240 teachers and tutors serving in nearly 50 local schools.

The Phoenix work is shaped by Arizona's education challenges, including teacher vacancies, uneven access to strong schools, and persistent gaps in student outcomes. Teach For America Phoenix emphasizes that Arizona needs leaders who are willing to work closely with students, families, schools, and communities to raise educational achievement and expand opportunity.

The regional program places corps members and tutors in classrooms serving low-income communities. Corps members commit to teaching while developing as leaders, and many alumni continue working in education after their initial commitment as teachers, school administrators, district leaders, policy advocates, nonprofit founders, entrepreneurs, and civic leaders.

Teach For America Phoenix also highlights the importance of Latino educational attainment in Arizona's future, given the state's demographic change and the disproportionate educational barriers faced by Latino students. Its local network aims to strengthen school and community partnerships, support educators, and build long-term leadership for educational equity across Phoenix and Arizona.

## Mission & Vision

Teach For America's mission is to find, develop, and support extraordinary leaders to transform education and expand opportunity for all children.

Teach For America Phoenix applies that mission locally by building a network of teachers, tutors, alumni, and community leaders committed to excellent educational opportunities for Arizona students. The region's work starts in classrooms but is intended to create broader systems change through leaders who continue serving students and communities across education, policy, nonprofit, business, philanthropy, and civic sectors.

The broader Teach For America vision is that one day all children will have the opportunity to attain an excellent education. The organization describes its desired future as one where educators, policymakers, parents, and students work together so children have the foundation they need to learn, lead, thrive, and shape a better future.
