# Save the Family Foundation of Arizona

Is an FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

Save the Family Foundation of Arizona is a Mesa-based nonprofit established in 1988 to help families address poverty, overcome homelessness, and achieve self-sufficiency. The organization is a leading provider of housing, case management, and supportive services for homeless and impoverished families throughout Maricopa County.

Save the Family works to ensure stable homes, safe children, and strong families. Its approach begins with housing stability, then pairs families with case management and wraparound supports that address the underlying barriers that led to homelessness. The organization serves families experiencing homelessness, families at risk of homelessness, and working poor families who need affordable housing and supportive services to remain stable.

The organization's housing programs include Rapid Re-housing, Tenant Based Rental Assistance, and Step Up to Independence. These programs are designed to meet the unique needs of each family and help them move toward long-term housing and financial stability. Save the Family also connects families to affordable housing through its Affordable Rental Movement program, which supports working families and individuals with affordable housing and services that help them maintain and increase self-sufficiency.

Save the Family's supportive services complement each family's case plan goals. Services include career development, budgeting classes, parenting classes, life-skills classes, mental health support, substance abuse counseling, and other resources that help adults build stability and move toward independence. Case managers work with parents throughout their journey to self-sufficiency, helping them set goals, address barriers, secure employment, improve financial literacy, and strengthen family stability.

The organization also provides child-focused services designed to interrupt cycles of homelessness and poverty. Children in Save the Family programs may receive support with necessities, school supplies, individual education plans, counseling, tutoring, after-school activities, and other tools that help them heal from the trauma of homelessness and reach their full potential.

## Mission & Vision

Save the Family's mission is to equip families to address poverty, overcome homelessness, and achieve self-sufficiency.

Its vision is communities free from family homelessness, where every person has a safe, affordable place to call home.

The organization is guided by core values that include anti-racism, integrity, compassion, excellence, sustainability, respect, and accountability. Across its housing and supportive service programs, Save the Family emphasizes dignity, individualized support, family stability, racial equity, safe and affordable housing, and the long-term goal of ending family homelessness.
