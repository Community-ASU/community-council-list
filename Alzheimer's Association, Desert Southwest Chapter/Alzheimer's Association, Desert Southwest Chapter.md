# Alzheimer's Association, Desert Southwest Chapter

Not an FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

The Alzheimer's Association Desert Southwest Chapter is the Arizona chapter of the Alzheimer's Association and serves people living with Alzheimer's disease and other dementias, along with their caregivers, family members, and communities across Arizona. The chapter provides support services, education, advocacy, and care navigation designed to help families better understand the disease and access resources.

The chapter is part of the national Alzheimer's Association network and focuses on public awareness, caregiver support, and programs that help people affected by dementia learn, plan, and connect with assistance. Its work includes local programs, a 24/7 helpline, community education, and efforts to improve access to diagnosis, treatment, and support.

Across its public materials, the chapter emphasizes dignity, support, and practical help for families facing memory loss. It also highlights volunteer and fundraising engagement as part of sustaining services across the region.

## Mission & Vision

The Alzheimer's Association's mission is to eliminate Alzheimer's disease through the advancement of research; to provide and enhance care and support for all affected; and to reduce the risk of dementia through the promotion of brain health.

Its vision is a world without Alzheimer's and all other dementia.

The Desert Southwest Chapter carries that national mission into local communities by providing education, support groups, care consultation, and advocacy. Its local work helps families navigate the realities of dementia while the organization continues to push toward better treatments, prevention, and ultimately a cure.
