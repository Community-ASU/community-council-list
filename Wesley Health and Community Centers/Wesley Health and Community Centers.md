# Wesley Health and Community Centers

Is an FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

Wesley Health and Community Centers, also known as Wesley Community & Health Centers, is an Arizona nonprofit community health organization serving Phoenix-area families and individuals. The organization is a multi-site Federally Qualified Health Center that provides primary medical care, behavioral health care, and community services for underserved populations in Central Phoenix and surrounding communities.

Wesley traces its work back to 1950, when Wesley Community Center began serving families in south central downtown Phoenix. The organization has grown from a neighborhood community center into an integrated health and community services provider with multiple campuses, including Phoenix, Golden Gate, Coffelt, and Recker locations.

Its health center services include family medicine, pediatric care, prenatal care, chronic disease management, sexual and reproductive healthcare, HIV and STI testing, health education, nutrition counseling, and behavioral health support. Wesley accepts many forms of insurance, Medicare, and AHCCCS, and offers sliding-fee discounts for eligible patients based on income and family size.

Alongside its healthcare work, Wesley continues to provide community-centered programs and services intended to support families, youth, and neighborhoods. Its overall model connects clinical care with prevention, education, wellness, and community support so that individuals and families can access help across multiple areas of need.

## Mission & Vision

Wesley's mission is to make measurable health improvements through client-centered and client-guided programs and services, the reduction of health disparities, and partnerships that can more holistically transform communities.

Its vision is to be a primary care home of choice that reflects community needs and empowers individuals, families, and neighborhoods to reach their full potential for health and wellbeing.

Wesley frames its work around the statement "Together We Empower Positive Change." The organization emphasizes accessible and affordable high-quality care, compassion, teamwork, respect, community voice, inclusion, integrated programs and services, transparency, innovation, education, training, sustainability, and financial stability.
