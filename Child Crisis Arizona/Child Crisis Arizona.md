# Child Crisis Arizona

FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

Child Crisis Arizona is a Phoenix-area nonprofit that has supported vulnerable children, youth, and families for nearly five decades. The organization focuses on helping children stay safe, strengthening families, and preventing future crises through a combination of prevention, intervention, and education programs.

Its services include emergency residential care, foster care and adoption, early education, counseling, resource distribution, family education, and support for teens and young adults. Child Crisis Arizona also operates a Center for Child & Family Wellness that expands its ability to provide holistic, community-centered support in Mesa and across the Valley.

The organization emphasizes that it works both during times of crisis and when crisis can be prevented. Its public materials describe a mission-driven model centered on safe kids and strong families, with services designed to build resiliency, stability, and opportunity for children and caregivers.

## Mission & Vision

Child Crisis Arizona's mission is to provide children and youth in Arizona a safe environment, free from abuse and neglect, by creating strong and successful families.

Its vision is safe kids and strong families.

The organization carries out that mission through prevention, intervention, and education programs. It also highlights values such as passion, growth, respect, positivity, sustainability, advocacy, and innovation, which guide its work with children, families, and community partners.
