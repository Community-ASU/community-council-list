# St. Mary's Food Bank

FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

St. Mary's Food Bank is an Arizona nonprofit hunger relief organization founded in Phoenix in 1967. It is recognized as the world's first food bank and was created by John van Hengel after he saw the need for a system where people and companies with surplus food could deposit it for distribution to families and individuals facing hunger.

The organization provides nutritious food to families, children, seniors, and other neighbors experiencing food insecurity throughout Arizona. St. Mary's Food Bank distributes food at no cost through hundreds of partner agencies, including food pantries, homeless shelters, meal centers, domestic violence shelters, afterschool programs for at-risk children, and low-income senior living centers.

St. Mary's Food Bank is one of the largest food banks in the world and serves communities across a large portion of Arizona. Its work includes food distribution, emergency food boxes, home delivery, children's feeding programs, food drives, water drives, volunteer packing and distribution, grocery rescue, food sourcing, and partner agency support. The organization uses its purchasing power and operations to turn each donated dollar into multiple meals for people in need.

The food bank partners with more than 600 nonprofit organizations to help Arizonans access food. It also works with grocery stores, retailers, manufacturers, farms, distributors, businesses, and community groups to rescue and distribute millions of pounds of food that might otherwise go unused.

St. Mary's also operates workforce development programming through its Skills Center, which helps adults gain practical job skills in warehouse logistics and food service. This work supports the organization's broader goal of not only meeting immediate hunger needs, but also helping people build tools for a more stable future.

## Mission & Vision

St. Mary's Food Bank's mission is bridging the gap for Arizona communities with nourishment for today and hope for tomorrow.

The organization carries out that mission by providing food assistance, supporting partner agencies, distributing meals and groceries, responding to emergency needs, operating feeding programs for children and seniors, rescuing food, mobilizing volunteers, and helping adults build job skills through workforce training.

Its vision is reflected in its commitment to ending hunger and building a food-secure community where neighbors have access to nutritious meals and the support needed for a better future. Across its work, St. Mary's emphasizes nourishment, hope, dignity, community partnership, volunteerism, operational efficiency, and practical support for Arizonans facing food insecurity.
