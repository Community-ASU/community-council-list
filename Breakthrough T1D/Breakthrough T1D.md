# Breakthrough T1D

Not an FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

Breakthrough T1D is the leading global organization focused on type 1 diabetes research, advocacy, and support. Founded in 1970 by families whose loved ones had been diagnosed with type 1 diabetes, the organization has played a major role in advancing research, policy, and access to care for people living with the condition.

The organization recently evolved from the JDRF brand to Breakthrough T1D, reflecting both its long history and its current focus on accelerating life-changing breakthroughs to cure, prevent, and treat type 1 diabetes and its complications. Its public materials describe a broad mission that includes connecting the brightest minds, advancing research, influencing policy, and improving everyday life for people who face T1D.

In Arizona, the organization operates through its Desert West chapter, which serves as a local hub for events, support, advocacy, and community engagement across Arizona and nearby regions. The chapter provides local staff support, fundraising activities, education, and community connection for people affected by type 1 diabetes.

Breakthrough T1D's work spans research, advocacy, education, and community building. Its approach includes support for clinical trials, policy efforts, local chapter programming, and resources that help families and adults living with T1D find community and practical support.

## Mission & Vision

Breakthrough T1D's mission is accelerating life-changing breakthroughs to cure, prevent, and treat type 1 diabetes and its complications.

Its broader purpose is to make everyday life better for the people who face T1D while advancing toward cures. The organization describes a long-term vision of a world without type 1 diabetes, supported by research, access, and collaboration across the scientific, healthcare, policy, and community ecosystems.

The organization frames its work around bold scientific progress and practical impact. That includes supporting the T1D community, accelerating research pathways, and improving access to emerging therapies and care models so people with T1D can live healthier lives now while the field moves toward cures.
