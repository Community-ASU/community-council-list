# Valley Leadership

Not an FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

Valley Leadership is an Arizona nonprofit leadership development organization that prepares, connects, and mobilizes leaders to take action on the state's most pressing issues. The organization has been strengthening Arizona leaders for more than 45 years and focuses on helping people build the knowledge, relationships, skills, and perspective needed to create meaningful community impact.

At its core, Valley Leadership builds community by empowering a broad spectrum of Arizonans to lead through leadership programs and by activating alumni and partners through Impact Maker. Its programming is designed for people at different stages of their leadership journey, from emerging leaders to experienced professionals who want to expand their impact across Arizona.

Valley Leadership's leadership development work includes programs such as Catalyze, Explore, and Corporate. Catalyze helps participants grow their connections, learn from community and industry leaders, examine their leadership style, and develop a plan for action. Explore takes participants across Arizona to understand the state's diverse regions, communities, histories, systems, and challenges. Corporate programming provides interactive workshops for business and organizational leaders who want to lead with integrity, purpose, and impact.

The organization also operates Impact Maker, a collaborative space that brings Valley Leadership alumni, community champions, subject matter experts, and partners together to create and execute strategic solutions to issues that matter most to Arizona. Impact Maker focuses on long-term change and gives participants a pathway to move from leadership learning into collective action.

Valley Leadership's approach is grounded in its Principles of Doing leadership framework. The framework emphasizes integrity, team mindset, building trust, putting Arizona first, and being driven to do. These principles guide how the organization develops leaders, engages communities, and works with its network to move Arizona forward.

## Mission & Vision

Valley Leadership's mission is to empower, leverage, and mobilize leaders to meaningfully impact the most pressing issues facing Arizona.

Its vision is a broad spectrum of Arizonans working together to impact the issues that matter most and setting the example on leadership.

The organization's values include diversity, equity and inclusion, history, partnerships, and fun. Valley Leadership emphasizes respect for individual differences, learning from Arizona's history, building impact through collaboration, and strengthening the social capital that comes from relationship-building across its statewide network.
