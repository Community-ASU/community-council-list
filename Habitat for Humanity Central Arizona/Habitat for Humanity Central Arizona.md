# Habitat for Humanity Central Arizona

Not an FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

Habitat for Humanity Central Arizona is a locally run affiliate of Habitat for Humanity International serving families across Central Arizona. The organization builds, renovates, and repairs simple, decent, affordable homes for families of modest means, while also advancing neighborhood revitalization, aging in place, veteran services, and construction training.

The affiliate presents itself as a practical response to the affordable housing crisis in Maricopa and Pinal counties. Its public materials emphasize that safe, affordable housing helps families gain stability and create better outcomes for children, workers, and communities. Habitat also runs ReStore operations that support its mission while giving the public a way to donate and shop for reuse-based home improvement items.

Founded in 1985, Habitat for Humanity Central Arizona says it has spent more than 40 years bringing people together to build homes, communities, and hope. It highlights work with volunteers, donors, and partner organizations as a core part of its model and notes that it serves a wide range of families, including seniors, veterans, and single parents.

The organization also emphasizes financial transparency and local accountability as an independent nonprofit affiliate. Across its website, Habitat frames its role as building more than houses: it is building long-term stability, stronger neighborhoods, and opportunities for families to thrive.

## Mission & Vision

Habitat for Humanity Central Arizona's mission is to bring people together to build homes, communities and hope.

Its vision, reflected in public statements from the organization, is a world where everyone has a decent place to live.

The mission is carried out through affordable homeownership, critical repairs, neighborhood revitalization, and related community programs. Habitat's broader aim is to help families gain the stability that decent housing provides, while also engaging volunteers and community partners in a shared effort to address housing need.
