# Tanner Community Development Corporation

Not an FY25 funded partner of Valley of the Sun United Way.

## About

Tanner Community Development Corporation, commonly known as TCDC, is a Phoenix-based nonprofit focused on transforming communities through compassion, connection, service, health, education, and empowerment. The organization works with individuals and families across the greater Phoenix area, offering resources, mentorship, and programs that promote growth, stability, wellness, and opportunity.

TCDC's work is organized around core initiatives that address social determinants of health and support underserved communities. Its Health & Wellness work prioritizes prevention, education, mental well-being, and healthy lifestyle choices. Through community partnerships and prevention strategies, TCDC works to help residents make informed decisions and improve positive life outcomes.

The organization's youth programming includes Youth Taking Charge and substance misuse prevention efforts through the HEAAL Coalition. These programs support young people ages 12 to 17 with education, mentorship, leadership development, and tools to navigate peer pressure, bullying, and risky behaviors. HEAAL, which stands for Help Enrich and Advance Lives, focuses on reducing youth substance misuse and strengthening community awareness, advocacy, and prevention.

TCDC also supports seniors through programs that promote active aging, social connection, digital literacy, transportation access, and participation in community events such as farmers markets. Its transportation work helps seniors stay connected to resources, activities, and community life.

The organization provides assistance with SNAP and energy assistance navigation for individuals and families with limited income. TCDC helps community members understand application requirements and access benefits that can support food security and household stability.

TCDC's property development work expands the organization's focus into affordable housing. In collaboration with Tanner Properties, TCDC is working to develop affordable, high-quality housing throughout the greater Phoenix area, including housing and community resource support connected to veterans. This work reflects TCDC's belief that stable housing is a foundation for healthier families, stronger neighborhoods, and more resilient communities.

## Mission & Vision

TCDC's mission is to uplift individuals and families by offering resources, mentorship, and programs that promote growth, education, and empowerment.

The organization describes its broader mission as uplifting, equipping, and empowering individuals and families across the greater Phoenix area through inclusive, innovative, and intentional programs that create healthier and more connected communities.

TCDC's vision is reflected in its commitment to be a beacon of hope for the community by providing help, healing, education, outreach, and practical support. Its guiding ideas include reaching in to educate and develop individuals and families, reaching out as an outreach arm into the greater Phoenix community, and reaching up to support people in communicating their needs and personal goals.
